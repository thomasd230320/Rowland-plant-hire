# Updating the website

A plain-English guide to changing prices, tools and photos on
rowlandplant.co.uk. No technical knowledge needed.

## Signing in

Go to **rowlandplant.co.uk/keystatic** and sign in with GitHub.

You only need to do the GitHub bit once. It is free, and it is what proves
it is really you making the changes.

## How saving works

When you press **Save**, the change is recorded and the website rebuilds
itself. Give it about a minute, then refresh the page to see it live.

Nothing you do here can break the site permanently. Every change is kept, so
anything can be undone.

## Changing a hire price

1. Click **Tool hire categories** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click the category the tool is in, e.g. *Concrete Breaking*.
3. Scroll to **Products** and click the tool.
4. Under **Hire rates**, type the new price. Include the pound sign: `£19.00`.
5. Press **Save**.

Leaving all four rate boxes empty makes the website show *Call for price*
instead — useful for anything priced on enquiry.

## Adding a new tool

1. Open the category it belongs in.
2. Under **Products**, click **Add**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Product name** — what customers should see, e.g. *Petrol Disc Cutter 12"*
   - **Photo** — click to upload one from your computer
   - **Specifications** — click *Add* for each bullet point
   - **Hire rates** — the four prices, or leave blank for *Call for price*
   - **Internal reference** — a short lowercase name with hyphens instead of
     spaces, e.g. `petrol-disc-cutter-12`. It is never shown to customers; it
     just has to be different from every other tool.
4. Press **Save**.

## Removing a tool

Open the category, find the tool under **Products**, and use the remove
button next to it. Then **Save**.

If you are only out of stock temporarily, it is better to leave the tool
listed — customers still search for it.

## Gas bottles

Click **Gas bottles** in the left-hand menu. There is one entry per type of gas
— Patio & BBQ, Caravan & Camping, Trade & Plumbers, and Welding — and each
holds its own list of bottles.

### Changing a gas price

Open the section, find the bottle, and fill in whichever boxes apply:

- **Gas / refill price** — what the customer pays for the gas itself.
- **Refundable bottle deposit** — for everything except welding gas. This is
  the deposit they get back when they bring the empty bottle in.
- **Cylinder hire** — welding gas only, where the cylinder is on hire rather
  than on deposit. Write it out in full, e.g. `£60.00 / year`.

Leave a box empty and it simply does not appear. Leave all three empty and the
website shows *Call for price*.

### The "prices are a guide" notice

Until the real prices are confirmed, the gas page carries a notice asking
customers to ring. To remove it, go to **Company details** and untick
**Gas prices are a guide only**.

Do that once, when the prices on the page are genuinely right.

### Naming the gas supplier

**Company details → Gas supplier name.** Fill it in and the gas page mentions
who supplies the gas. Leave it blank and no supplier is named.

### Changing the order of the sections

Each gas section has a **Position on the page** number. 1 shows first, 2
second, and so on.

## Changing a photo

Click the existing photo and choose a new file. Landscape photos work best.
Keep files under about 1MB so pages stay quick to load on a phone.

## Changing the words on a category page

Each category has an **Intro paragraph** — the text under the heading. This
is worth getting right: mentioning Witney and the surrounding villages is
part of what gets you found on Google.

There is also a **Highlighted note** box for things you want to stand out,
like *"Sharpening charge per chisel @ £3.75 applies to all breakers."*

## Changing how the page looks on Google

Under **Google search listing** on each category:

- **Google title** — the blue clickable line. Keep it under about 60
  characters or Google cuts it off.
- **Google description** — the grey text underneath. Aim for under 155
  characters.

Google can take a few days to pick up changes.

## Phone number, email and opening hours

Click **Company details** in the left-hand menu. Note there are two phone
fields: one as you want it written on screen (`01865 922611`), and one for
the tap-to-call button on mobiles (`+441865922611`, no spaces).

## Two things to leave alone

- **Web address** — changing it breaks existing links and loses the page's
  place in Google.
- **Advanced — leave this alone** — this is how the website keeps track of
  each item behind the scenes. Safe to set when creating something new,
  but never change it on something that already exists.

## If something looks wrong

Every change is saved separately and can be undone. Nothing is ever really
lost, so don't panic — just ask.
